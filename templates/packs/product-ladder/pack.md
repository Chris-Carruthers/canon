# Pack: product-ladder

> The cheap rungs below a full spec — one-pager, PR-FAQ, six-pager, Shape Up pitch.

## What this is for

canon's core ships one product document, `Product Spec`, and it is the heavyweight
one: five sections in a fixed order, machine-checkable blocks, sequential IDs. That is
the right artefact for funded work and the wrong first artefact for an idea nobody has
agreed on yet. Writing it takes long enough that finishing it feels like progress, and
it produces acceptance criteria for something that may not survive its first reading.

This pack is the rungs underneath.

| Template | Cost | Write it when |
|---|---|---|
| One-pager | ~30 min | An idea is worth a decision but not yet a plan |
| PR-FAQ | ~2 hours | You need to know whether anybody will care before you build |
| Six-pager | ~half a day | A real decision is being made and the room must reason, not react |
| Shape Up Pitch | ~2 hours | The problem is agreed and the question is what fits the appetite |

## When not to install this

If your team already has a product-document format its readers expect — a client's, a
regulator's, a predecessor's everyone imitates — these will compete with it. Two formats
for the same job is worse than either.
