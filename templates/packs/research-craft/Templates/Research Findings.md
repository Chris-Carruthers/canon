WHY THIS EXISTS: without it, research survives as remembered anecdote. Somebody
says "users hate that flow" and nobody can reconstruct whether that was six people
or one loud one, or when, or what they were actually shown.

STATE FINDINGS AT THE CONFIDENCE THEY WERE OBTAINED AT. Five participants is five
participants. Writing "users want X" from five conversations is not a summary, it is
an upgrade — and the upgrade is invisible to everybody downstream who then treats it
as settled.

n IS IN THE FRONTMATTER FOR A REASON. It travels with every quotation of this note.

A QUOTE-DUMP WITH A CONCLUSION BOLTED ON IS NOT A FINDINGS DOCUMENT. The quotes are
evidence for a claim. State the claim, then the evidence, then how confident you are
and why.

## What we did not learn

The questions this did not answer, and the ones it raised. An honest gap is a
finding; silence reads as coverage.

## What would change our mind

The observation that would overturn the main finding. If nothing could, it was not
research.

## Where this is being used

Which decisions cite it. Findings that inform nothing should be reconsidered, or
retired.

## Limitations

Recruitment bias, sample size, what the setting made impossible to see. Written
plainly, not as a disclaimer paragraph nobody reads.
