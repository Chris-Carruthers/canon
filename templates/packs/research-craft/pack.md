# Pack: research-craft

> Research findings, design critique, and a content style guide.

## What this is for

Three documents that shape what gets built without describing it.

**Research Findings** is the one most often skipped, and its absence is expensive in a
specific way: without it, research survives as remembered anecdote. Somebody says "users
hate that flow" and nobody can reconstruct whether that was six people or one loud one,
or when.

**Design Critique** gives structured feedback a place to live. Verbal critique
evaporates, and the same objection then gets raised in three separate reviews.

**Content & UX Copy Guide** is what stops interface text from being negotiated per
string. Most teams have strong opinions about copy and no written rule, so the same
argument recurs and whoever cares most that day wins.

| Template | Write it when |
|---|---|
| Research Findings | You talked to people and the conclusions should outlive your memory |
| Design Critique | A design was reviewed and the reasoning should not evaporate |
| Content & UX Copy Guide | Interface text is being argued about more than once |

## When not to install this

Research Findings assumes somebody does research. If nobody does, the honest move is to
say so in a `Reference/` note rather than to install a template that will stay empty and
imply otherwise.

## Voice

`skills/house-voice/references/research-findings.md`, `design-critique.md`,
`content-style-guide.md`. Overridable at `Reference/Writing/House Voice.md`.
